# RE:ENTRY demo script

The shortest judge-friendly path is a two-minute tour of the visible safety
boundary:

1. Open the seeded **Flood recovery / Case 042** case. Point out the six
   evidence records and the $460 contradiction; RE:ENTRY refuses to guess.
2. Click **Review** on **Submit emergency housing packet**. Show the three
   citations in the checkpoint, then click **Approve & send**. The row becomes
   completed and a mock receipt appears in the audit/timeline.
3. Click **Test response** on **Prepare insurer loss package**. The connector
   rejects the draft; the response becomes evidence and **Request signed
   inventory affidavit** appears behind a new approval checkpoint.
4. Open **Repair estimate** in the evidence drawer to show the exact excerpt,
   confidence, and tags. Finish on the **Why this is safe** panel and the
   **Agent path** strip.

All connector calls are seeded local mocks. No real agency, insurer, resident,
or bank is contacted by the demo.
